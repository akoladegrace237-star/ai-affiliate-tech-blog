# 📊 Business Plan: AI Affiliate Tech Blog

> **Prepared:** March 2026 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** Active

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Business Name:** AI Tech Reviews  
**Business Model:** Affiliate Marketing Blog  
**Niche:** Tech & Gadgets  
**Revenue Model:** Affiliate commissions, display ads, sponsored content  
**Startup Investment:** ~$50–$200/month  
**Projected Revenue (Month 12):** $2,000–$8,000/month  

### Vision
Build the #1 AI-powered tech review blog that helps 1 million readers make smarter purchasing decisions annually, generating $100K+ in affiliate commissions.

### Mission
To produce honest, high-quality, AI-assisted tech product reviews at scale — faster than competitors, with lower cost, and without sacrificing accuracy.

## 3. Revenue Model

### Primary Revenue Streams

#### 3.1 Affiliate Commissions (Target: 70% of revenue)
| Network | Category | Commission Rate | Est. Monthly Revenue (Month 12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amazon Associates | All tech | 2.5–8% | $1,200 |
| ShareASale | Software, accessories | 10–25% | $600 |
| Impact | Premium brands | 5–15% | $400 |
| CJ Affiliate | Electronics retailers | 3–10% | $300 |

**How it works:**
1. User searches "best wireless headphones 2026" on Google
2. Finds our review article (ranked in top 3 via SEO)
3. Clicks affiliate link to Amazon
4. Buys the product (any product, within 24 hours)
5. We earn 2.5–8% commission

#### 3.2 Display Advertising (Target: 20% of revenue)
- **Mediavine** (requires 50K sessions/month): ~$10–$20 RPM
- **AdThrive**: ~$15–$30 RPM
- Target: 100K monthly pageviews = $1,000–$3,000/month in ad revenue

#### 3.3 Sponsored Content (Target: 10% of revenue)
- Sponsored product reviews: $300–$2,000 per post
- Brand partnerships: $500–$5,000/month
- Newsletter sponsorships: $200–$500/issue

## 5. Traffic Strategy

### Month 1–3: Foundation
- Publish 60+ articles targeting low-competition keywords
- Build topical authority in 2-3 subcategories
- Set up Google Search Console and Analytics

### Month 4–6: Growth
- Begin ranking for low-competition keywords
- Pinterest strategy (500+ pins targeting "tech gifts", "best gadgets")
- YouTube Shorts from review content (AI voiceover)
- First email subscribers

### Month 7–12: Scale
- Target medium-competition keywords
- Guest posting on tech sites for backlinks
- Reddit/Quora presence — answer tech questions, link to reviews
- Paid social media tests ($100/month budget)

## 8. Scaling Plan

### Phase 1 (Months 1–6): Build
- Launch site with 50+ quality articles
- Establish topical authority in 2–3 categories
- Reach $1,000/month revenue

### Phase 2 (Months 7–12): Grow
- Expand to 200+ articles
- Launch email newsletter (target 5,000 subscribers)
- Apply to Mediavine for display ads
- Reach $3,000–$5,000/month revenue

### Phase 3 (Year 2+): Scale
- Hire freelance human editors for quality control
- Expand to adjacent niches (home appliances, fitness tech)
- Build social media presence (Pinterest, YouTube)
- Target $10,000+/month

### Phase 4 (Year 3+): Exit or Expand
- Blog at $100K+/year revenue can be sold for 30–40x monthly revenue
- Potential sale value: $250,000–$400,000
- OR expand into SaaS (AI review tool for other bloggers)
