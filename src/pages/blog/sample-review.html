Frequently Asked Questions

Are the Sony WH-1000XM5 worth the price in 2026?

Yes, absolutely. While ~$349 is a significant investment, the WH-1000XM5 offers the best combination of noise cancellation, sound quality, and battery life available. If budget allows, they're worth every dollar.

How do they compare to AirPods Max?

The Sony outperforms AirPods Max in noise cancellation and battery life (30 hrs vs 20 hrs). AirPods Max offer slightly better spatial audio for Apple users and a more premium build. For non-Apple users, the Sony is the clear winner.

Can I use them for gaming?

You can, but Bluetooth latency may be noticeable in competitive gaming. For casual gaming and video watching, they're excellent. For competitive gaming, a wired headset with lower latency is recommended.

Are they waterproof or sweat-resistant?

No. The WH-1000XM5 has no IP rating and is not designed for workouts or wet conditions. If you need water resistance, consider the Sony WF-1000XM5 earbuds (IPX4 rated) instead.

What is the best budget wireless headphone alternative?

The Anker Soundcore Q45 (~$60) is our top budget pick. It offers surprisingly good noise cancellation, 50-hour battery life, and solid sound quality at a fraction of the price.
